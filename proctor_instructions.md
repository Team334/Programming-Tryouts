# Proctor Instructions!

## Before Starting
- Read the overview.
- Read the auto routine.
- Explain the simulated motor.
- Explain the important util functions.
- Clarify kinematics stuff (to wheel speeds, etc).
- Explain file structure.

## During Tryouts
- Make sure to help them out as much as you can if they are struggling.
- They should be explaining their thought process throughout.
- They can look anything up (and if they are copying code, make sure they actually understand it).

## At The End
- Ask about their personal project.
- Ask why they want to join programming?
- (any other questions?)
- Copy and paste the drive, intake, shooter, and robot files into a google doc, then go to source control and delete all changes for the next person.
